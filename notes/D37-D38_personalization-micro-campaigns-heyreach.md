# D37–D38: Personalization beyond {first_name} + AI personalization at scale (5 Oct 2026)

Source: Gemini summary and "Next steps" (screenshots). The full transcript wasn't available, so for the live HeyReach setup demo, watch the recording.

## TL;DR
- **Micro-campaigns beat mass AI personalization.** Small, tightly segmented campaigns keep message quality under control and make results trackable. With one big AI-personalized blast, you can't tell which message worked.
- **LinkedIn automation runs on HeyReach.** It automates connection requests and message sequences and pulls every reply into one inbox ("Unibox").
- **Safe limits per LinkedIn account:** about **25 connection requests a day** and **40 messages a day**. Split the **~750 requests a month** across **4 campaigns** to test different data and segments.
- **Fix the profile before outbound:** photo, headline and description, and education. Post thought-leadership content to build credibility. Buffer schedules LinkedIn posts (about $23 a month).
- **LinkedIn gets ~30% replies, against ~1–4% for email** (Yogesh's figures).
- **Segment by context, not seniority:** an event or situation (a hiring surge, a new role, a launch) gives a reason to talk. A title alone doesn't.

## Key points
**The market for GTM engineers**
- There are far more GTM-engineer candidates than job postings right now.
- Large enterprises split the work into specialised roles (sales engineering, RevOps). Early-stage companies are where a new practitioner gets to run real outbound end to end.

**Micro-campaigns and HeyReach**
- Prefer several small campaigns, each with one segment and one message, over one big AI-personalized campaign. You can see what works and copy it.
- HeyReach runs the LinkedIn sequence (profile visits, connection requests, follow-up messages) and collects replies in a single inbox.
- Respect the daily limits (25 requests, 40 messages) to avoid LinkedIn restrictions or bans.
- Yogesh's plan: split the ~750 monthly connection requests into **4 campaigns**, each testing a different data source or segment.

**Profile and content**
- Before any outreach, optimise the sender profile: picture, headline and description, education. Prospects check you out before they accept.
- Thought-leadership posts position you as an expert, so connection requests get accepted more often. Buffer can schedule them.

**Segmentation and testing**
- Segment on **context-driven data** (events, situations) rather than seniority level alone.
- Mass AI messaging makes performance analysis impossible: you can't tell which message drove a reply.
- LinkedIn automation shows measurable results **within about a month** across varied segments; getting a campaign right takes **2–3 months** of iteration.
- Many creators fail by launching campaigns without a clear strategic purpose.

**AI messaging and tools**
- Automated messages are easy to spot, which is why human creativity still matters.
- Gagan's workflow: automated profile visits plus manual message personalization, with a feedback loop into a database that generates tailored messages.
- **Trigify was acquired by HubSpot** and no longer offers free LinkedIn scraping.
