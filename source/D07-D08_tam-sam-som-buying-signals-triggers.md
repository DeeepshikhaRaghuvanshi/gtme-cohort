TAM, SAM, and SOM Framework

TAM (Total Addressable Market) encompasses the entire universe of potential customers fitting the Ideal Customer Profile.

SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market) is the subset of TAM reachable via specific geography, product, and sales channels.

SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market) represents the realistic market slice obtainable within a period, influenced by pricing, competition, and regional constraints.

SOM derivation requires qualitative understanding of market conditions rather than solely relying on database filters.

Signals, Intent, and Triggers

Signals are public company-level data points, such as funding rounds, hiring spikes, or social media statements, used for account scoring.

Intent represents person-level engagement, such as pricing page visits or content interaction, indicating specific interest.

Triggers are automated outreach workflows initiated when tools detect specific user intent.

RB2B captures person-level visitor data only within the US due to compliance and legal restrictions.
