Decision Makers versus Champions: Yogesh Jaiswal differentiated between decision makers and champions. A decision maker is the person with the authority to buy the product, such as a Chief Product Officer, while a champion is an individual who influences the deal but may not have purchasing authority. Engaging champions, such as product directors or managers, is a valid strategy when a decision maker is unresponsive (00:24:43).

This list should include direct competitors, active customers, and companies already in discussions with the sales team (00:27:24).
