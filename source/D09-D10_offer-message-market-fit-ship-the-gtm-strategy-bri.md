GTM Engineering Role and Scope

Yogesh defined GTM engineering as the post-strategy execution phase, involving automation, cadence building, and tooling like Apollo, Prospio, and Clay.

Strategic planning and initial copywriting remain the owner or founder's responsibility, while the GTM engineer focuses on operationalizing the messaging.

LinkedIn is preferred for targeted, pattern-based outreach, whereas email requires high-volume mass outreach and acts as a secondary channel.

Effective GTM engineering requires a comprehensive understanding of the client's industry, product-market fit, and current sales maturity.

Client Discovery and Knowledge Base Development

Yogesh emphasized creating a central knowledge base in Claude containing ICPs, meeting notes, and messaging to train AI for prospect interactions.

Essential client discovery questions include product status, current sales channels, deal cycle length, blockers, and competitive differentiation.

Engagements with clients having ambiguous positioning or unready products must be scoped as experimental phases, avoiding explicit ROI guarantees.

Information gathering should target specific deal hurdles, such as security reviews, procurement cycles, and previous sales performance.

Data Sourcing and Terminology Standards

Yogesh advised using SIC and NIC codes in research tools to bypass industry filtering limitations and exclude non-profit organizations.

Research data must be cross-verified against external benchmarks; manual debugging is necessary if tool outputs deviate significantly from market reality.
