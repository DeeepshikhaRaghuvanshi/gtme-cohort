Abi and Deepu have 4+ years as working professionals (full-stack/data engineering). They are new to GTM concepts, not to software or browsers. Write guides as a lean fast path to the deliverable: no keyboard-shortcut or bookmark tutorials, no optional tool signups on the critical path.

**Why:** On 2026-09-28 the playbook listed LinkedIn, Loom and Sales Navigator signups under the dedicated gmail. Abi thought he had to create a new LinkedIn, hit errors, and lost time on a deadline day. He called it inefficient and asked for the "bs" to be removed.

**How to apply:** Explain GTM concepts fully, since that's the part that's new to them. Keep execution steps terse. Say explicitly which identity each tool uses (personal accounts like LinkedIn and Loom stay on her real account). Put anything not needed for today's deliverable in a separate "later" section.
